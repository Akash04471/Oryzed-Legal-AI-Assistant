About Oryzed

Making Legal Technology More Accessible.

Oryzed is a Legal Tech AI startup building practical and accessible AI-powered tools for the legal community.

We believe that access to technology should not be limited by the size of a law firm, the institution a student belongs to, or the ability to afford expensive legal research platforms.

Oryzed was started with this idea to make legal technology more accessible to students, researchers, lawyers, and legal professionals across India.

What We're Building

We are developing AI-powered tools designed specifically for legal work:

  • AI Legal Research — Search and explore case laws, statutes, and legal principles using natural language.
  • Contract Drafting AI — A tool designed to assist with creating and customising legal contracts using structured legal resources.
  • Judgment Summarization — Convert lengthy judgments into concise and easy-to-understand summaries, helping users save time during legal research.

Our legal research tool is currently available as a working prototype, while our contract drafting and judgment summarization tools are under development.

Built for the Legal Community

Oryzed is particularly focused on making advanced legal technology accessible to students, independent researchers, small law practices, and lawyers working in district and lower courts, while building towards wider adoption across the legal ecosystem.

We aim to keep our tools accessible during our early growth stage and develop an affordable model as the platform grows.

Law × AI × Accessibility

Oryzed is being built at the intersection of law and technology.

We bring together legal researchers, law students, developers, AI enthusiasts, and professionals who are interested in exploring what AI can do for the legal sector.

We believe the best legal technology is built collaboratively with technology supporting legal professionals rather than replacing them.

Our Vision

To build an accessible legal AI ecosystem that makes legal research, drafting, and understanding simpler, faster, and more accessible.

Building the future of legal technology, together.
