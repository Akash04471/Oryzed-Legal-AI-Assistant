Part 2: Google Sign-In — The Only Data We Collect at Login

2.1 Google-Only Authentication

Oryzed does not operate a separate username/password system. Account creation and login are performed exclusively through "Sign in with Google" (Google OAuth 2.0). We do this deliberately: it removes the need for Oryzed to ever store your password, reduces credential-theft risk, and gives you a faster, more familiar, and more secure login experience.

2.2 What We Receive From Google

When you authenticate with Google, Google shares with us — with your consent, granted through Google's own consent screen — only the following limited data:

  • Your Google Account name
  • Your Gmail address
  • Your Google Account profile picture (if available)
  • A unique Google Account identifier (used solely to recognize your Account on return visits)
  • An OAuth authentication token (used solely to verify your session; we do not receive or store your Google password at any time)

We do not request, and Google does not share with us, access to your Gmail inbox, Google Drive, Google Calendar, Google Contacts, or any other Google service or data, unless you separately and explicitly opt in to a specific integration in future and are shown a distinct consent screen for that purpose.

2.3 Why We Use Google Sign-In

We use Google Sign-In solely to: (a) verify your identity and authenticate your login; (b) create and personalize your Oryzed Account (e.g., displaying your name and profile picture); (c) communicate with you at your Gmail address regarding your Account, billing, and material changes to our Services; and (d) prevent fraudulent account creation and misuse of the platform. We do not use your Google Account Data for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to any third party.

2.4 Deletion of Google Account Data

If you delete your Oryzed Account, all Google Account Data associated with that Account (name, Gmail address, profile picture, and identifiers) will be permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized from our active systems within thirty (30) days, save for the limited exceptions in Section 5.4 below. Deleting your Oryzed Account does not affect your underlying Google Account, which remains fully within your control via your Google settings. You may also revoke Oryzed's access to your Google Account at any time directly through your Google Account's "Third-Party Apps & Services" security settings.

3.1 User Content

If you subscribe to a paid plan, limited billing details (such as billing name, address, and transaction identifiers) are collected. Full card/payment credentials are processed directly by our PCI-DSS-compliant payment processor and are never stored on Oryzed's own servers.

IP address, browser and device type, operating system, access timestamps, referring/exit pages, and usage/interaction logs, collected automatically for security, fraud prevention, debugging, and service-improvement purposes.

We infer only an approximate, coarse location from your IP address for security and fraud-prevention purposes (e.g., detecting anomalous logins). We do not collect precise geolocation (such as GPS data) without your separate, explicit, opt-in consent.

We use strictly necessary cookies for authentication/session management and security, and limited analytics cookies to understand aggregate platform usage. We do not collect biometric data, health data, or data from any Google service beyond the login fields listed in Section 2.2, and we do not knowingly collect any data from individuals under the age of 18 (see Part 10).

Part 6: Artificial Intelligence — How Our Models Work and Their Limits

6.1 AI Model Training and Improvement

We may use User Content to test, improve, and train Oryzed's AI systems. Whenever User Content is used for this purpose, it is first aggregated and/or de-identified/anonymized using reasonable technical measures, so that it is not, in the ordinary course, reasonably capable of being linked back to an identifiable individual. You may withdraw consent for use of your User Content in model training/improvement at any time via Account settings or by writing to our Grievance Officer, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal; withdrawal may limit certain personalization features.

6.2 Confidential and Privileged Information — Your Responsibility

Because Oryzed operates in the legal domain, User Content may include highly sensitive, privileged, or client-confidential material. While we implement industry-standard encryption, access controls, and security safeguards, no system connected to the internet can be guaranteed to be completely impenetrable. You are strongly advised, and it is your responsibility, to redact or anonymize privileged, sensitive personal, or client-identifying information before uploading any document to the Services, unless such upload is strictly necessary and you have independently obtained any consents required (e.g., client consent) for doing so.

6.3 Probabilistic Nature of AI — Hallucinations

Oryzed's AI features are built on large language models that generate output by statistical pattern-matching over training data, not through human legal reasoning, judgment, or verification. Consequently, AI Output may be incomplete, outdated, or factually incorrect, and may include fabricated citations, case law, statutory references, or facts (commonly known as "hallucinations").

6.4 Mandatory Independent Verification

You must independently verify, using primary and authoritative sources, any AI Output before relying on it, filing it, sending it to a client or third party, submitting it to a court or authority, or otherwise using it in any professional, legal, or personal capacity. Oryzed AI Output is a drafting and research aid only and is never a substitute for independent professional judgment.

6.5 No Automated Legal Decision-Making

Oryzed does not use AI to make any legally or similarly significant automated decision about you or any third party without meaningful human involvement. All AI Output is advisory and assistive in nature only.
